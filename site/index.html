About

I am drawn to things that invite experimentation. Video games, music software, guitar pedals, and interactive art have all shaped the way I think about technology. I enjoy using these tools, but I am equally interested in how they are designed and built.

I studied Media Arts and Film before earning a Computer Science degree at Montclair State University. That background taught me to consider both how software works and how people experience it.

I also work as a freelance developer, building and maintaining websites for nonprofit clients.
