Hey!

My background is in university advancement and nonprofit fundraising.

At Columbia University, I wrote and audited reports, managed data quality and governance, and provided technical support for the Alumni & Development office's advancement system containing more than five million active constituent records.

Those reports covered The Columbia Commitment (2016–2021): from more than $2 billion and about 100,000 donors at the May 2017 public launch, through close announced in 2022 at $5.6 billion raised, 199,000 donors, and 160,000 engaged alumni—above the $5 billion, 173,000-donor, and 150,000-engaged-alumni goals—along with 103,000 first-time donors.

Prior to Columbia, I supported constituent record data quality and processed gifts at the Parkinson's Foundation.

I currently work as a freelance developer, building and maintaining websites for nonprofit clients on a pro-bono basis.

I am looking for a full-time software engineering role, either remote or based in the New York metropolitan area.

About
